# Product Vision: "StudySync AI"

## 1. Overview

**StudySync AI** is an intelligent academic workspace designed to transform how students prepare for exams and collaborate on coursework. It acts as a dedicated "study assistant" that bridges the gap between vast amounts of course material and the specific, high-stakes questions found in exams.

At its core, the app moves beyond simple file storage. It leverages a powerful Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system to "read" and "understand" all uploaded documents, such as lecture notes, textbooks, and past papers.

## 2. The Core Problem

Students are often overwhelmed by the volume of information (e.g., hundreds of pages of notes) they need to review for a single exam. When faced with a complex exam problem, they waste valuable time manually searching for relevant formulas, concepts, or examples, with no guarantee of finding the most helpful information.

## 3. The Solution: AI-Powered Search

StudySync AI's primary function is an intelligent search engine. A student can input an exam problem, a key concept, or a question. The system will:
1.  **Analyze** the query.
2.  **Scan** all relevant course materials in that student's (or their group's) workspace.
3.  **Surface** a list of the *most relevant passages* and excerpts from the documents that are key to solving that problem.

Crucially, the UI is optimized for **an active learning workflow**, not just finding. It will support this by:
* Allowing users to **'Preview'** relevant context without losing their place.
* Providing direct links to **'Go to Page X'** in the full source document for deeper study.
* **Presenting a 'General Chat' tool alongside the 'Passage Finder,'** making it seamless for students to find a key passage, then immediately copy it into the chat to ask for a simpler explanation, a summary, or a related example.

## 4. Beyond Search: Collaborative Study Spaces

While the AI assistant is the innovative core, the application is built around **Shared Study Spaces**. This is a workspace feature, similar to a simplified Microsoft Teams or Slack, but with a focus on shared academic content rather than just social connection (Notion being a similar app in terms of colaborative capabilities also).

In these spaces, students in the same class or study group can:
* Upload and share a common library of documents.
* Run AI-powered searches on this shared library.
* View and discuss the AI-generated results, ensuring the whole group is "on the same page."
* Have a central hub for all their study materials, accessible from anywhere.
